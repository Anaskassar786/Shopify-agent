## PART 8: Future Roadmap, AI Agents, MCP, Enterprise Expansion

> Status: RECEIVED (2026-08-05)

## VISION
Evolve from Shopify app into an **AI Business Operating System** for e-commerce. AI acts as a Digital Business Manager — merchant opens Shopify and the AI has already: analyzed business, found opportunities, generated recommendations, executed approved automations, created reports, tracked revenue impact, prepared tomorrow's strategy.

## AI COPILOT / CONVERSATIONAL AI (future phases)
Merchant asks: "Why are sales down?" · "What should I restock?" · "Who are my VIPs?" · "Which products are dying?" · "What discount should I offer?" · "Predict next month's revenue." Natural-language actions: show revenue, find churn risks, generate campaigns, create discounts, summarize business.

## MULTI-LLM SUPPORT
One interface → Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, open-source LLMs, future providers. Merchant may choose provider if supported.

## MCP (Model Context Protocol)
Design for MCP compatibility; secure external-tool connection. Future: documents, knowledge bases, ERP, CRM, analytics platforms.

## AI AGENTS (future multi-agent system)
CEO · Business Analyst · Marketing · Inventory · Pricing · Customer Success · Support · Finance · Operations — each with defined responsibilities, communicating through controlled workflows.

### Autonomous workflow examples
Low inventory → Inventory Agent → demand prediction → purchase recommendation → merchant approval → supplier workflow (future).
High churn risk → Customer Agent → generate campaign → AI writes email → merchant approves → campaign sent.

## PREDICTIVE AI

## PRICING OPTIMIZATION
