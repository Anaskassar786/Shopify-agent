# PROFIT TOOL AI — MASTER PRD
## PART 11: Business Model, Pricing, Customer Acquisition & SaaS Growth Engine

> Status: RECEIVED (2026-08-05)

## OBJECTIVE
Scalable recurring-revenue SaaS: convert trials → paying customers, retain long-term, increase LTV, grow predictably.

## BUSINESS MODEL
Primary: subscription SaaS (monthly/yearly). Revenue sources: monthly subscriptions · annual plans · enterprise contracts · agency plans · add-on features · API access (future).

## PRICING STRATEGY
Value-based, not low-price. Positioning: "AI Revenue Optimization Platform." Sell outcomes, not features (e.g., "Recover lost revenue automatically using AI" — not "AI Recommendations").

## PLAN STRUCTURE (example pricing)
- **Free Trial:** 3 days. Dashboard, basic AI recommendations, store analysis, limited automation preview. Goal: convert.
- **Starter:** $19–29/mo — dashboard, basic analytics, AI recommendations, customer insights, limited automation, monthly reports. Target: small merchants.
- **Growth:** $49–99/mo — Starter + unlimited recommendations, automation workflows, email recovery, customer segmentation, advanced analytics, AI campaign suggestions. Target: growing stores.
- **Professional:** $199–299/mo — Growth + predictive analytics, discount intelligence, inventory AI, multiple users, priority support, advanced reports. Target: serious merchants.
- **Enterprise:** custom — multiple stores, custom AI agents, dedicated support, API access, custom integrations, SLA. Target: large brands.

## TRIAL CONVERSION JOURNEY
- Day 0: install → connect store → initial AI analysis.
- Day 1: "Your store has 12 opportunities."
- Day 2: "AI found potential $4,500 recovery opportunity."
- Day 3: trial ending → "Upgrade to continue recovering revenue."

## ACTIVATION METRIC
Merchant activated when: store connected · first AI insight viewed · first recommendation approved · first automation enabled.

## ACQUISITION CHANNELS
1. Shopify App Store (most important — optimize title, description, screenshots, reviews, keywords, ranking)
2. Content marketing (blog posts on merchant pain points)
3. YouTube (demos, case studies, tutorials)
4. Social (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit)
5. Partnerships (Shopify agencies, developers, marketing agencies, e-commerce consultants)
- Affiliate program (future): 20–30% recurring commission.

## RETENTION SYSTEM
- **Daily AI value:** "Today's business opportunities" (churn-risk customers, low stock, revenue opportunities).
- **Weekly reports:** revenue summary, AI insights, growth suggestions, automation results.
- **ROI reporting:** before/after revenue, cart recovery, conversion, improvement %.
- **Churn prevention:** detect low usage / no login / no automation / no recommendations viewed → education email, success stories, support offer.
- **Customer success:** knowledge base, tutorial videos, setup guides, FAQ.

## IN-APP MONETIZATION (future add-ons)
Extra AI credits · extra automation runs · additional stores · advanced reports.

## SAAS METRICS
MRR · ARR · churn · LTV · CAC · activation rate · trial conversion. Healthy unit economics: LTV ≫ CAC (example: $100/mo × 24 months = $2,400 LTV vs $300 CAC).

## ADMIN BUSINESS DASHBOARD (owner)
Revenue · MRR · ARR · new installs · trials · paid users · churn · AI usage · costs · profit.

## SCALING STAGES
1. 100 merchants — product-market fit
2. 1,000 — automation, support, marketing
3. 10,000 — enterprise, team, infrastructure
4. 100,000+ — global platform

## BUSINESS RISKS & MITIGATIONS
1. Too many features before customers → launch MVP, collect feedback.
2. AI doesn't create measurable value → track revenue impact.
3. High AI costs → optimize model usage + caching.
4. Merchants don't trust automation → explainable AI + approval workflow.

## FINAL GOAL
Merchants pay because the platform directly helps them earn more revenue, recover lost customers, make better decisions, automate repetitive work.
