# Demo Video Script — App Store Submission (≤ 2 minutes)

Recorded at submission time on the dev-store capture rig (see
`screenshot-plan.md`). One continuous screen recording, no actors, no stock
footage, no fabricated metrics — every number on screen is computed by the
app from the seeded dev store.

| Scene | Duration | On screen | Narration (voiceover) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0:00–0:10 | Shopify admin → Apps → **PROFIT TOOL AI** opens fullscreen | "PROFIT TOOL AI runs entirely inside your Shopify admin — no other login." |
| 2 | 0:10–0:25 | Install on a fresh store: OAuth consent → onboarding wizard → first sync progress | "Install to first insight in minutes: OAuth, scopes you can read, then a guided onboarding that starts syncing your store's own data." |
| 3 | 0:25–0:45 | Dashboard with real numbers; hover revenue trend; attributed AI revenue card | "The dashboard answers one question — what did my store earn, and how much of it did AI-driven actions contribute." |
| 4 | 0:45–1:05 | AI Recommendations: open one, rationale + expected impact + confidence visible; approve it | "Every recommendation shows its evidence. Nothing executes without your approval unless you say otherwise." |
| 5 | 1:05–1:25 | Automation builder: open cart-recovery workflow, show delay + branch, toggle on | "Automation is a visual workflow — delays, conditions, guardrails like per-store discount caps. You design it, the engine runs it." |
| 6 | 1:25–1:40 | Campaigns: live stats (sent/opened/clicked), unsubscribe note | "Recovery campaigns with real tracking — no guesswork about who saw what." |
| 7 | 1:40–1:55 | Billing: trial countdown, usage meters, plan comparison; then Audit logs filtered to AI actions | "Transparent billing and a complete audit trail. Every AI action, every operator action — on the record." |
| 8 | 1:55–2:00 | Wordmark master (`docs/appstore/brand/logo-wordmark.svg`) + "Available on the Shopify App Store" | "PROFIT TOOL AI — your AI revenue and automation manager for Shopify." |

## Production notes

- Record at 1920×1080; keep the browser at 100% zoom; hide the bookmarks bar.
- Export MP4 (H.264, ≤ 30 fps, ≤ 100 MB) per Shopify's media upload spec.
- The seeded dev store shoots 1–8 in ONE take order; scenes 5–7 can be
  re-shot independently without touching the order data.
- If a scene ever requires faking a state (mocked API response, edited
  number), the take is scrapped — reseed the dev store instead.
